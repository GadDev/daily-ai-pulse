Copilot for JetBrains 1.18 introduces assisted approvals: low-risk tool calls can be approved automatically, while higher-risk operations still require a human decision.

## Why it matters

This is a practical version of **risk-tiered autonomy**. Permission systems for coding agents are getting more granular than a binary “ask for everything” versus “full access.”
