## What changed?

Pulse is moving from a single daily digest into a small AI publication built around individual stories, durable archives, and a curated daily front page.

## Why this structure?

Important research, tools, engineering practices, real-world deployments, and curious AI stories should not disappear inside one oversized daily post.

## Pulse take

The publication should feel authored, technical, and useful — not like another feed of press releases.
