Today’s edition follows four points where an AI system’s behavior becomes inspectable. Uber treats an agent’s purpose as an enforceable lifecycle contract; Kakao narrows repeated agent choices into bounded probabilities; a new preprint puts the fallible voter inside the multi-agent reliability model; and GitHub exposes code-review policy and reported provenance through APIs. Each development adds a control surface, but none removes the need to validate the surrounding system.
