Android Studio's Bring Your Own Agent feature allows external coding agents—including Claude Agent, Codex, and Antigravity—to operate through Android Studio's optimized infrastructure and tools.

## Why it matters

IDE vendors are moving from bundling one assistant toward becoming **agent execution platforms**. That reduces lock-in and lets teams keep their preferred harness while reusing IDE-native Android context and tooling.
