## 1. Big Walk says AI touched under 0.1% of its code. The argument is much bigger.

**New event and date:** 2026-11-10. New reporting of House House's public disclosure; the precise FAQ edit date is unknown. This is not the August game launch.

**Memorable verified detail:** House House describes a roughly ten-line rendering fix after extensive human modification, while some IGF jurors oppose generative AI in any capacity.

**What Pulse would add:** Compare the studio's explanation with the awards dispute: what does AI-assisted mean when code, art and authorship are being judged together?

**Evidence limit / unresolved questions:** No independent code audit establishes the percentage. Do not describe an IGF-wide ban or confirmed disqualification.

**Suggested treatment:** Reported story, 500–650 words. Model pitch recommendation; no copy has been commissioned or written.

**Evidence inspected:** [big](https://bigwalk.game/faq/); [aftermath](https://aftermath.site/big-walk-ai-igf-jurors-controversy/).

**Comparison with the news rubric:** Engineering relevance is plausible through the rendering example; the cultural awards dispute is the Curious angle. No numerical news score assigned.

## 2. AI helped owners fix their gadgets. Now some report getting locked out.

**Memorable verified detail:** 404 Media documents user reports of reverse-engineering blocks and quotes an OpenAI engineer saying there is no general prohibition and accidental blocks are being fixed.
