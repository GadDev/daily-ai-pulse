Microsoft's new Autopilot is designed as a persistent agent that can watch channels, follow up, run recurring work, and resume projects days later without waiting for a fresh prompt.

It lives in the enterprise tenant with its own identity, memory, computer, and workspace. Copilot Managed Runtime provides a hosted execution layer for custom solutions.

## Why it matters

Persistent agents force enterprise platforms to answer identity, memory, permissions, audit, and cost questions that short-lived chat assistants could defer.
