K-Bench challenges a convenient assumption in machine unlearning: if the final answer no longer reveals a fact, the system has forgotten it.

The benchmark observes six channels exposed by a ReAct-style agent, including tool observations and summaries. The authors report that secrets can remain recoverable even when answer-only benchmarks say they are gone.

## Why it matters

Privacy and deletion guarantees need to be evaluated at the **agent-system level**. Retrieval stores, prompts, traces, and tool outputs can preserve knowledge that the base-model evaluation misses.
