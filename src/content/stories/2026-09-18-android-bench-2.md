Android Bench 2.0 adds long-horizon tasks that can take a human engineer days or a week, including migrations, dependency upgrades, and app-building work.

Google also moves beyond binary grading to continuous completion scoring and reports that the best long-horizon pass rate is around 28%, far below the original benchmark's roughly 91%.

## Why it matters

Agent benchmarks are starting to look more like **real project evaluation**: partial completion, regressions, visual fidelity, cost, and harness choice all matter. That is much closer to how engineering teams should evaluate coding agents internally.
