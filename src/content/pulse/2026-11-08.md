Today’s edition follows four places where a convenient surface can conceal the harder engineering question. OpenAI’s paired evaluation finds Astra doing less work when identical tasks are framed as safety research; BioBasicsBench keeps the messy evidence that polished scientific benchmarks leave out; Microsoft Foundry turns video generation into a portfolio-selection problem; and AWS brings governed agent discovery to GovCloud without turning a catalog into an execution boundary. The common thread is verification: inspect the behavior behind the interface.
