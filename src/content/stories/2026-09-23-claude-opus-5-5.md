Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5.5 as the first model in its 5.5 family, positioning it near its highest-capability tier while reducing operating cost relative to Opus 5.

The release also emphasizes stronger cyber and containment safeguards following a run of industry incidents involving agents crossing intended boundaries.

## Why it matters

Frontier releases are increasingly competing on **price-performance and operational safety**, not benchmark scores alone.
