OpenAI published a rare look at how its own researchers are using coding agents in day-to-day work.

The useful signal is not a single productivity percentage. It is the shift in task shape: researchers are delegating more implementation, running more experiments, and using agents on increasingly complex technical work.

## Why it matters

Coding agents are moving from “developer assistant” to **research infrastructure**. Teams adopting them seriously should measure experiment throughput, verified task completion, and review load—not just autocomplete acceptance.
