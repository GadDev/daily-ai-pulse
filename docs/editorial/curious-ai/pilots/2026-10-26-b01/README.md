## Dates and what this pilot cannot establish

The television story is an explicitly dated catch-up on October 20 reporting and earlier examples.
The other four are evergreen explanations with their older dates made visible: Botober's
September announcement remains relevant during October; Lagos asks a durable hiring question;
school literacy separates an older policy from later examples; Minecraft revisits a saved lead
after finding its primary account. None is presented as a new October 26 event.

This is consequently a stronger test of evergreen selection than of daily breaking-news supply.
It does **not** demonstrate that seven fresh, worthwhile stories exist every day. It also does
not test a full feature, artwork, translation, production reservations, site publication or deployment.
Source access constrained several timely candidates. A larger audience claim would require
reader assessment; five drafts reviewed by their author are not that assessment.

## Evaluation and next decision

The exact-copy [review record](REVIEW.md) binds the author's claim/title/opening assessment to
SHA-256 for every sample. That is model self-review, not independent editorial approval.
Structural validators test the ledger, arithmetic, copy hashes, ownership and repository integrity;
they cannot test whether a story is fair, delightful or worth a reader's time. CI observations belong
in the draft PR body and must name the actual current head.

Maintainer assessment should record, for each sample:

1. Any unsupported fact, missing qualifier or misleading title/opening; any material error blocks promotion.
2. Source traceability and whether the piece adds a useful question instead of substituting for the source.
3. Interest and comprehension, each 1–5, plus the one thing the reader remembers.
4. Keep, revise or drop, with the reason; compare with the excluded candidates, not only the selected set.
5. Actual editing minutes and changes needed; distinguish human time from model wall-clock time.
6. Whether the portfolio is sufficiently varied and whether older material feels honestly dated.

No human/reader results are recorded yet. Recommendation: **ready for pilot assessment,
not ready to promote production**. Review the examples and access-related watch decisions first.
Leave rollout at pilot. Subsequent passes should inspect this same PR/CI and wait for assessment;
do not generate another pilot or more samples merely because another day begins.
