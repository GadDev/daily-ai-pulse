Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei argued that frontier AI companies should deliberately slow capability advancement enough to improve evaluation, coordination, and safety controls.

The interesting part is not the rhetoric; it is the proposed operating model: independent safety evaluation, coordination among frontier labs, and stronger international cooperation.

## Why it matters

For engineers, this debate eventually becomes concrete requirements around release gates, model evaluations, incident reporting, and deployment controls. Governance is increasingly moving closer to the development lifecycle.
