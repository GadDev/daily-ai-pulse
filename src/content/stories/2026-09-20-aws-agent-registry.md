AWS Agent Registry provides a private catalog for agents, tools, skills, MCP servers, and custom resources, with semantic search, approvals, CloudTrail audit trails, cross-account sharing, and automatic discovery of AgentCore resources.

## Why it matters

Once an organization has hundreds of agent capabilities, discovery and ownership become platform problems. A governed registry reduces duplicate tooling and gives security teams a place to reason about what exists.
