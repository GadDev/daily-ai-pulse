1Password says its core Codex cohort improved engineering productivity by about 21% and reduced median pull-request cycle time by nearly 11%.

The implementation is more interesting than the headline number: Codex is used for feature work, review, tests, production investigation, and internal tools, while credentials stay behind 1Password's security controls.

## Why it matters

The case study reinforces a practical pattern: agent adoption works best when it is integrated into an existing delivery and security system rather than bolted on as a separate chat tool.
