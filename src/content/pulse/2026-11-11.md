Today’s edition follows five systems that turn an abstract AI promise into an operational boundary. OpenAI puts its research inference behind a continuously checked stop control; Anthropic splits one model family across distinct capability and safeguard tiers; an OpenAI–Upwork study measures where freelance demand is shifting while leaving causality unresolved; Google packages speech recognition and synthesis for offline use; and Businesses in ChatGPT introduces verified profiles and agents without yet proving discovery outcomes. The common question is not whether the feature exists, but what remains enforceable and measurable once it meets production.
