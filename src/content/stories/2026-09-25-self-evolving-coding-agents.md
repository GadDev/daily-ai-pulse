A new survey organizes work on coding agents that change themselves from prior interactions: updating memory, skills, tools, framework behavior, models, or collaboration structures.

The field's advantage is also its risk. Software provides executable feedback, but bad feedback can create benchmark overfitting, brittle memory, or unsafe persistent behavior.

## Why it matters

Self-improvement needs the same disciplines as deployment: **reversibility, evaluation, provenance, and controlled promotion of learned behavior**.
