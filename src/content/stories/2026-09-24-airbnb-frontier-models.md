Airbnb says its engineers use an internal AI tooling platform to write software and create remote agents across several OpenAI model tiers, and has expanded access to GPT‑6 Astra.

## Why it matters

Large organizations increasingly treat models as **replaceable backends behind an internal agent platform**, rather than asking every developer to choose vendors and integrations independently.
