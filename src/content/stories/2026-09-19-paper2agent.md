Paper2Agent converts research papers and their associated code into MCP servers with tools, resources, and prompts, then connects them to an agent interface.

The system validates generated tools against reference outputs before packaging them. In the Nature paper, 593 of 599 proposed tools across 100 computational-biology papers passed automated validation.

## Why it matters

This is more than RAG over PDFs. It treats a paper as an **executable research artifact** whose methods can be reproduced and reused through a standardized agent interface.
