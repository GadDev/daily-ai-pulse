# Pulse Product Direction

The Daily AI Pulse is a curated AI engineering publication for software
engineers. Its product promise is **signal over noise**: standalone, sourced
stories gathered into a short daily edition, with evidence quality and
engineering consequences visible to readers.

The [PRD](PRD.md) owns audience, product requirements, information architecture,
and roadmap. The [README](../README.md) is the entry point for running the
site and understanding the current publishing workflow. Editorial decisions
follow the [decision engine](editorial/DECISION_ENGINE_V1.md); the current
publication structure follows the [content model](CONTENT_MODEL.md).

Keep changes to product requirements in the PRD rather than repeating
taxonomies, formats, or evidence rules here.
