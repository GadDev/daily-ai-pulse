Salesforce introduced a long-horizon runtime for Agentforce so an agent can pursue a business goal over days or weeks instead of a single chat session.

The platform highlights three primitives: memory that preserves context, durable execution that resumes work, and dynamic steering that lets a user redirect an ongoing plan.

## Why it matters

Long-running agents need explicit lifecycle semantics. Persistence, resumability, approvals, and course correction become product requirements once the unit of work lasts longer than one interaction.
