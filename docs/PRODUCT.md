# Pulse — Product Requirements v1

## Vision

Pulse is a personal AI engineering publication for software engineers who want current signal without reading dozens of announcements every day.

## Homepage

A curated daily front page with five editorial slots:

1. Big Story — optional, only for genuinely important developments.
2. Research.
3. Tools.
4. In Practice.
5. Curious.

Each item links to a standalone story page. Category archives retain everything else.

## Editorial desks

- Models & Releases
- Research
- AI Engineering
- Dev Tools
- AI in Practice
- Workflows
- Business & Industry
- Curious AI

## Story depth

- Pulse: 150–300 words
- Briefing: 500–900 words
- Deep Dive: 1,500–3,000 words

## Evidence

- strong — independently reproduced
- primary — vendor/lab source
- preliminary — preprint
- anecdotal — practitioner report
- unverified — treat cautiously

## Voice

Approximately 75% evidence/explanation and 25% personal editorial voice. Facts and opinion remain visually distinct.

## Publishing

Research → verify → score → dedupe → draft → PR → human review → merge → GitHub Actions → GitHub Pages.
