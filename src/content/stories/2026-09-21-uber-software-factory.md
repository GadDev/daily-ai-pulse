Uber says more than 70% of pull requests are attributed to local or cloud agents, with more than 3,600 internal agent skills and over 30,000 skill executions per day.

The workloads go well beyond code generation: review, self-healing CI, visual E2E validation, on-call triage, debugging, and maintenance are increasingly agent-initiated.

## Why it matters

At this scale, agent adoption becomes a platform discipline: reusable skills, policy, cost control, attribution, and escalation matter more than individual prompting tricks.
