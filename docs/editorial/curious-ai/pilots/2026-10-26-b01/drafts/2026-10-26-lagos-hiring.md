# A Lagos makeup artist used ChatGPT to prepare for her first hire

*A September profile offers a different view of AI at work: software helping a small-business owner prepare to employ a person.*

The next step for a Lagos makeup business was hiring a human. Its owner asked a chatbot how to prepare.

In [Rest of World's September 14 collection of worker profiles](https://restofworld.org/2026/global-workers-using-ai-at-work/), Nureeyah Ogunmuyiwa describes using ChatGPT to develop interview questions and a checklist for her first employee. The package, credited to Rest of World Staff, includes Damilare Dosunmu's reporting on her business.

Ogunmuyiwa told the publication that the preparation helped her hire an assistant. The useful detail is the task she delegated: help organizing an unfamiliar process. The account does not establish that the chatbot independently selected the employee, or that its suggestions were objectively the best hiring method.

The profile also records her unease about consulting AI before making every decision. This is a revisit of a September report, not a newly measured October result. One entrepreneur's experience cannot establish a productivity gain for a city, an industry or small businesses generally. We have not interviewed Ogunmuyiwa or audited her business.

**Pulse interpretation:** the story gives the replacement debate a more precise question. What does the owner want help doing? Writing a checklist and deciding whom to trust are different responsibilities. Putting them under the same label—AI hiring—would obscure the human choice that makes this account interesting.

The outcome worth noticing is modest and concrete: a business owner says she used software to prepare for a working relationship with another person.
