## Editorial illustration production briefs

These are **art direction inputs, not illustrations or approved generation-identity records**. Build final publication-ready `src/content/stories/<id>.md` first, then derive canonical story ID and immutable brief, run `npm run illustration:decision`, and follow the existing illustration skill. Do not claim GENERATE/REUSE, model version, asset integrity or successful image review without actually running them.

Shared placement for each story: `article-hero`, wide crop. Reference: `docs/reference-layouts/story.html` and its shared assets. If the site contract confirms the dimensions, 1600 × 900 WebP is acceptable. All briefs require three image candidates, hard constitutional rejection before scoring, comparison with at least two actual golden production references, crop review and an exact story-linked 1.3 review record.

Shared art direction: understated commissioned editorial illustration, physical presence and material depth, one memorable silhouette, quiet scientific/architectural sensibility, muted print/paper texture; canvas #F3EBDD, paper #FAF6EE, ink #171B1A, gray #575D5B and sparing terracotta #8A4B35. **No** text, letters, numbers, logos, interface/screenshots, pictograms-as-diagrams, generated chart data, border, glow, robots, or stereotyped AI imagery. Maintain cover-crop readability.

### A. TV cleanup — a screen made simpler

- **Source fact:** Some owners use coding agents and community scripts to disable unwanted packages and replace crowded launcher experiences on certain TVs. Public projects document ADB-based methods and device-specific limits.
- **Editorial idea:** Owners remove layers that manufacturers added.
- **Metaphor:** A substantial, unbranded dark-glass screen emerges from several thick, peeling layers of paper and translucent lacquer; the screen remains blank, calm and open, with removed fragments falling as *material*, not app tiles or UI.
- **Archetype:** Mechanical / physical metaphor.
- **Must avoid:** Fake television interface, brand marks, shattered or destroyed electronics, guaranteed universal compatibility.
- **Reference comparison:** `public/images/stories/2026-09-28-claude-plugin-ecosystem.webp` and `public/images/stories/2026-09-22-copilot-review-controls.webp`.
- **Alt-text candidate:** An unbranded television screen revealed as layers of clutter peel away.

### B. Botober — strange racehorse names as creative prompts

- **Source fact:** Janelle Shane describes a small character-based neural network trained on a list of 42,000 registered racehorse names. Her September 30 Botober post shares odd prompts for people to interpret creatively.
- **Editorial idea:** Human imagination provides the meaning that a peculiar model output lacks.
- **Metaphor:** From a curled sheet of textured paper rises an imperfect horse-shaped sculpture assembled from flowing ink, torn offcuts and folded fabric; intentionally whimsical but physically cohesive, no printed names.
- **Archetype:** Scientific specimen / artifact study.
- **Must avoid:** A literal prompt list, race results, champion portrayal, labels or equestrian branding.
- **Reference comparison:** `public/images/stories/2026-09-24-greedy-decoding-precision.webp` and `public/images/stories/2026-09-14-looped-flows.webp`.
- **Alt-text candidate:** A whimsical horse-shaped paper sculpture emerging from unmarked sheets.

### C. Lagos hiring — assistance, not replacement

- **Source fact:** Lagos makeup artist Nureeyah Ogunmuyiwa says she used ChatGPT to prepare interview questions and a hiring checklist for her first employee. She describes the result as hiring a human assistant and has expressed concerns about depending too heavily on AI.
- **Editorial idea:** A tool supports a decision; it does not become the person being hired.
- **Metaphor:** On a beautifully restrained makeup artist's worktable, a fan of makeup brushes rests beside two hand-finished adjacent stools; one empty sheet of translucent paper helps form the bridge between them without becoming a software screen.
- **Archetype:** Tool / artifact study.
- **Must avoid:** Portrait implying likeness of a real person, fake chatbot interface, claims of automated employee selection.
- **Reference comparison:** `public/images/stories/2026-09-28-claude-plugin-ecosystem.webp` and `public/images/stories/2026-09-24-gpt-6-sol-luna.webp`.
- **Alt-text candidate:** Makeup brushes and two work stools beside a folded sheet of unmarked paper.

### D. UAE AI literacy — learning to instruct before logging in

- **Source fact:** Educators in Dubai describe teacher-led exercises in which younger pupils practice precise instructions or identify an incorrect AI answer. A separate May 2025 policy announcement introduced AI in UAE public-school curricula.
- **Editorial idea:** Understanding a system can precede direct access to one.
- **Metaphor:** A sunlit tabletop hosts a playful, tactile sculpture made from folded paper strips guiding an unmarked clay form; successive folds become more deliberate, with no depicted robot, chatbot or child at a computer.
- **Archetype:** Mechanical / physical metaphor.
- **Must avoid:** Robots, fake classroom screenshots, pictograms resembling an infographic, fabricated classroom outcome.
- **Reference comparison:** `public/images/stories/2026-09-24-greedy-decoding-precision.webp` and `public/images/stories/2026-09-14-looped-flows.webp`.
- **Alt-text candidate:** Folded paper pathways guide a simple clay form on a classroom-like tabletop.

### E. Minecraft — the tool-assisted dragon challenge

- **Source fact:** An October 15 preprint reports four Gemini 4 Argon agents defeating Minecraft's Ender Dragon in a single run. The agents used Mineflayer and an unusually informative programmatic interface; the run was on Easy difficulty.
- **Editorial idea:** A notable achievement only makes sense alongside the tools and constraints.
- **Metaphor:** Four weathered, tactile stone towers converge beneath the *shadow* of a dragon across an abstract blocky landscape, seen as a sectional print with unmistakable depth. Do not imitate a Minecraft screenshot or portray the exact run as witnessed.
- **Archetype:** Editorial network / sculptural environment.
- **Must avoid:** Game logos, game HUD, numbers, agents as robots, claim of guaranteed generalization.
- **Reference comparison:** `public/images/stories/2026-09-28-deepmind-agent-swarm.webp` and `public/images/stories/2026-09-27-agent-trace-tampering.webp`.
- **Alt-text candidate:** Four stone towers gathered under a dragon-shaped shadow in an abstract block landscape.
