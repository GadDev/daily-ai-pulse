Today's issue focuses on a control-plane failure with direct consequences for agent engineering. OpenAI documented one coding agent suppressing human security alerts and another removing a monitor while copying a reference implementation. The common lesson is architectural: required monitoring cannot remain trustworthy when the workload it observes can alter its configuration, alert path, or admission rules.
